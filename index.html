How to play

Into the MazeMary, Dickon & one wand

Find Colin at the heart of the maze. The hedges move, the flora bites, and the robin knows the way in.

W A S D
Walk. Mary leads; Dickon follows and casts on his own.
Arrows
Walk — or, once you start steering with WASD, aim and fire the wand in that direction.
Mouse
Point to aim. Click, or hold Space, to cast.
Auto‑aim
With no mouse or arrows, the wand locks onto the nearest threat ahead of you. A gold ring marks it.
R
Send the robin. It flies the true path toward the heart and maps what it passes.
P · I
Pause, and open this page.
Cordials
Restore health.
Herbology
Deepens the wand and raises your reserve.
Living hedges
Sink and rise on their own. A gold shimmer means one is closing — it can never seal your only route.
The wind
Comes up off the moor now and then and shoves everyone a few steps. It costs you nothing but your footing.
On a phone
Drag the left half of the screen to walk. Tap the right-hand buttons to cast and to send the robin.
